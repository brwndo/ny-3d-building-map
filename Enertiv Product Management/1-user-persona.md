# User Persona: Sustainability/ESG Officer

## Profile

Sustainability/ESG Officer (or Director of Sustainability) at a real estate owner/operator or investment manager overseeing an industrial portfolio — often dozens to hundreds of properties, many triple-net leased. Reports to asset management and investors/LPs, and answers to regulators (local benchmarking laws, disclosure rules). Usually part of a small team, or a team of one, covering a portfolio too large to visit property by property. Much of the underlying data comes from tenants who have little incentive to share it.

## Primary Job-to-Be-Done

Prove — to investors, regulators, and internal leadership — that the portfolio's energy, water, and emissions data is complete and accurate, and that the portfolio is on a credible path to its decarbonization targets, without having to chase tenants or reconcile utility bills by hand.

## Primary Concerns / Tensions

- **Trust vs. verification gap**: their real anxiety isn't "what's our carbon number" — it's "can I trust this number, and can I defend it if audited."
- **Data coverage in NNN properties**: tenant-controlled meters are the single biggest point of failure in industrial portfolios.
- **Credibility is personal risk**: if reported data is wrong or a filing is missed, it reflects on them directly to investors and regulators.
- **Understaffed relative to scope**: small teams covering large portfolios means little time for manual reconciliation or property-by-property investigation.
- **Reporting vs. execution gap**: they're measured on compliance and trajectory, but often lack the authority or bandwidth to drive the capital projects that would move the numbers.

## Daily Tasks / Where Time Is Spent

- Chasing tenants and property teams for utility data, especially in NNN buildings
- Reconciling utility bills and manually filling data gaps
- Investigating anomalies or flagged variances to determine if they're real or data errors
- Preparing reporting packages for investors, LPs, and regulatory filings (ENERGY STAR, LEED, local benchmarking laws)
- Assembling documentation and evidence in advance of or during audits
- Tracking certification status and renewal deadlines across the portfolio

**Time sink**: most time goes to data verification and gap-filling rather than analysis — chasing down whether a number is real before they can act on or report it.
