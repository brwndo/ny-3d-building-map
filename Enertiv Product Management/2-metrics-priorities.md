# ESG Dashboard: Metrics Priorities

Ordered to match the persona's actual sequence of concerns: can I trust this data → am I exposed → are we hitting our targets → where's it going wrong → what's the market-facing status.

## 1. Data Coverage / Completeness Rate
% of properties and floor area with verified utility data, broken out by tenant-controlled vs. landlord-controlled meters.
**Why first**: every other metric is only as credible as this one. It's the biggest failure point in industrial/NNN portfolios and the root of the "can I trust this number" tension.

## 2. Regulatory / Compliance Exposure
Properties out of compliance with local benchmarking laws, estimated fine exposure, upcoming filing deadlines.
**Why**: this is the "what happens if I do nothing" metric — drives urgency and budget, and is the most direct personal risk to the officer.

## 3. Carbon Emissions & Progress Against Net Zero Targets
Absolute emissions (kg CO₂e) shown together with trajectory vs. CRREM/net-zero pathway.
**Why**: the core reporting deliverable for investors and LPs. Shown together rather than separately, since the raw number and the trend tell one story, not two.

## 4. Energy Consumption Intensity (kWh/sqft)
Normalized energy use per property, benchmarked across the portfolio.
**Why**: standard metric for ENERGY STAR scoring and investor comparison. Kept distinct from emissions since fuel mix can move one without moving the other.

## 5. Water Usage vs. Baselines
**Why**: distinct reporting cycle and methodology from energy (often ordinance-driven), and a distinct data-collection challenge in industrial portfolios (irrigation, process water, cooling).

## 6. Energy / Water Variance by Property (Heatmap)
Year-over-year or vs.-benchmark variance, flagged for review.
**Why**: the diagnostic layer under #4 and #5 — surfaces which properties are moving the wrong direction without requiring a manual review of every property.

## 7. Certification Progress (ENERGY STAR, LEED)
Status tracker: certified / expiring / eligible-not-pursued.
**Why**: a lagging, status-based metric rather than a live one, but a common question from investors and leasing teams — belongs on the dashboard as its own tile type.

## 8. Decarbonization Opportunity Value
ROI-ranked list of pending projects with cost, savings, and emissions impact.
**Why**: lowest priority for a monitoring-focused dashboard. Ties reporting to execution, but is more central to an asset manager or decarbonization-lead view than the ESG officer's core monitoring job.
