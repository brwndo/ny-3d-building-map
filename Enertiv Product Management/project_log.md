## 2026-08-09 19:20 UTC — ENERGY STAR eligibility split from Data Coverage
`energyStarEligible` was computed from the 1-100 score alone (`score >= 75`), so 11 of 30 assets were labelled "certification eligible" in the Detail Panel - including NY-017 (score 90) and NY-019 (score 80), both with Confidence Tier `Missing`, and NY-028 (score 88) at 57.5% coverage. Real eligibility needs a second, independent gate: 12 full consecutive months of whole-building data across all fuel types. That requirement was already captured in `4-research-esg-frameworks-compliance.md` but never implemented. Added a `12-Month Whole-Building Data Complete` (Y/N) column to Asset-Level Data (column AE, 22 Y / 8 N) and redefined eligibility as `score >= 75 AND 12-month complete`, dropping the eligible count to 8. Kept it deliberately independent of Data Coverage % - coverage answers "how much is verified" for audit-defensibility, the flag answers "does a complete year exist"; the data now demonstrates both directions (NY-023 at 93.8% coverage marked N, NY-026 at 48.2% marked Y). Detail Panel now names the failed gate rather than silently dropping the eligible label. Eligibility stays Detail Panel only - no filter, no map or color change; band verification unchanged at 146/180. Requirements doc gained §5.6 and two now-false claims were corrected (§5.3 and §5.4a both equated the ENERGY STAR `Target Met` band with eligibility).

Worth flagging: the workbook edit could not be done with openpyxl. The xlsx holds ~1,400 formulas *and* the cached results that `prepare_data.py` reads with `data_only=True`, and openpyxl preserves only one of the two - the first attempt silently wiped every cached value, leaving `Current Value`, `% Progress to Target` and `Status` as `None` across Baseline & Targets. Caught before it reached the geojson, restored from backup, and redone as direct sheet-XML surgery in `scripts/add_twelve_month_column.py` (idempotent, backs up first, verified byte-identical outside the new column). A `.backup.xlsx` copy now sits alongside the workbook.
